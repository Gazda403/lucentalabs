# Lucenta Labs — Luxury Skincare & Beauty Tech Storefront

A high-performance, editorial headless e-commerce storefront for **Lucenta Labs** (Luxury Beauty Tech & At-Home Skincare Devices).

## 💎 Features
- **Design Aesthetic:** Luxury minimalism inspired by *The Act*, *BLUNT*, and *Core Atelier*.
- **Hero Video & Visuals:** Seamless editorial hero with interactive technology hotspot pins.
- **Shopify Headless Checkout:** Integrated with Shopify live product variants and cart permalinks (`/cart/{variantId}:{qty}`).
- **Interactive Cart Drawer:** Slide-over cart with free shipping progress bar and instant checkout.
- **Editorial Experience:** 3-step ritual breakdown, clinical comparison table, verified client reviews, and FAQ accordion.
- **100% Mobile Optimized:** Sticky quick-buy bar and responsive layouts.
